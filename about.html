Our Story

Founded by Satyam and Lucky, Lucky Satya Auto Service Center started with a simple goal: to bring professional, honest, and affordable garage services directly to our local village community.

We realized that local vehicle owners had to travel miles to big towns just for routine maintenance, oil changes, or minor repairs. We built this workshop to bridge that gap with fast turnaround times and reliable work.
